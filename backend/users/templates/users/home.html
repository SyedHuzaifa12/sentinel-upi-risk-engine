Real-time UPI transaction risk scoring — allow / warn / review / block, decided before the money moves.

UPI fraud is overwhelmingly Authorised Push Payment fraud — the victim is socially engineered and willingly authorises the payment, so device, PIN, and location are all genuine. Detection has to come from payee-side behaviour (mule fan-in, account age, pass-through velocity) and payer deviation (first-ever payment to this VPA, amount z-score, collect-request initiation) — not credential verification.

All data here is synthetic. Fraud typologies are encoded by a generator, not observed — see Results below and the repository for what that means for the metrics.

Features are computed server-side from each payer/payee/device's own transaction history, never supplied by the caller. Point-in-time correctness is enforced at the history-store layer. A payee with fewer than 3 prior transactions routes to the stricter cold model; otherwise warm. Every decision gets a calibrated probability, a cost-minimizing action, and — for anything above ALLOW — per-transaction SHAP reason codes, never hardcoded explanation text.
