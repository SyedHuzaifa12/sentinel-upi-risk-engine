About UPI & Security Challenges

Understanding UPI technology and its security implications

What is UPI?

Unified Payments Interface (UPI) is an instant real-time payment system developed by the National Payments Corporation of India. It facilitates inter-bank peer-to-peer and person-to-merchant transactions.

Real-time Processing

Transactions are processed instantly, making fraud detection challenging.

Mobile-First Design

Designed primarily for mobile devices, increasing vulnerability to mobile-based attacks.

Security Challenges

Lack of physical verification increases risk of digital fraud.

Common UPI Fraud Patterns

Phishing Links

Fraudulent links sent via SMS/WhatsApp posing as payment requests.

Remote Access Scams

Fraudsters gain control of devices through screen-sharing apps.

SIM Swap Fraud

Fraudsters duplicate SIM cards to bypass OTP authentication.

QR Code Manipulation

Malicious QR codes that redirect payments to fraudster accounts.
