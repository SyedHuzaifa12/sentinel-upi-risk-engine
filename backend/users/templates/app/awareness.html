What is UPI Fraud?

UPI fraud refers to unauthorized transactions or financial scams conducted through the Unified Payments Interface system. Fraudsters use sophisticated techniques to steal money, personal information, or banking credentials from unsuspecting users.

These frauds typically occur when users are tricked into sharing sensitive information or when malicious software compromises their devices. With UPI's real-time transaction capabilities, fraudsters can instantly transfer funds once they gain access.

When Fraud Occurs

UPI frauds typically happen during vulnerable moments when users are less cautious or when security measures are temporarily bypassed.

During Transactions

When entering UPI PIN or approving payments

Clicking Links

Opening suspicious links in SMS/WhatsApp

QR Code Scans

Scanning malicious QR codes for payments

Common Types of UPI Frauds

Phishing Scams

Fake websites/apps mimicking legitimate banking portals to steal credentials

Remote Access Fraud

Fraudsters gain control of devices through screen-sharing apps

SIM Swap Fraud

Duplicating SIM cards to intercept OTPs and bypass authentication

QR Code Fraud

Malicious QR codes redirecting payments to fraudster accounts

Social Engineering

Impersonating bank officials or trusted contacts for information

App-based Frauds

Malicious apps with hidden charges or data theft capabilities

How to Protect Yourself

1

Never Share Credentials

Your UPI PIN, OTP, and passwords should never be shared with anyone, including bank officials

2

Verify Recipients

Always double-check the UPI ID and name before confirming any payment

3

Avoid Unknown Links

Don't click on suspicious links received via SMS, email, or WhatsApp

4

Secure Your Device

Use antivirus software and keep your operating system updated

5

Monitor Transactions

Regularly check your bank statements and transaction history

6

Use Official Apps

Only download banking and UPI apps from official app stores

Immediate Action Required

Emergency Contacts
  • National Cyber Crime Helpline: 1930
  • Banking Ombudsman: 14440
  • RBI Grievance: crpc@rbi.org.in
If Fraud Occurs
  • Report immediately to your bank
  • Change all passwords immediately
  • Block your UPI ID temporarily
  • File police complaint online
